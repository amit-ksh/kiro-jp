01

Begin with the learner

First demo audience

An English-speaking beginner

No assumed kana, kanji, or grammar knowledge. Predefined placement questions recommend a supported starting point; the learner can accept or adjust it.

Also supported

A WaniKani learner ready to use words

Optional import identifies familiar kanji and vocabulary. Grammar, listening, and speaking remain separate abilities.

02

One session, continued learning

The core idea connects remembered words to usable Japanese. Reviewed teaching defines the objective; one free-conversation mode follows the learner's chosen topic. Lessons suggest a topic and a goal, then open the same chat. Qloo enriches the discussion and can suggest conversations or roleplay.

01

Find a starting point

Sign in, select a level or “I'm not sure,” and answer predefined kana, vocabulary, grammar, and listening questions. Accept or adjust the recommendation. Choose interests during onboarding; update them later.

02

Review, then learn a small set

Recall due meanings and readings. Learn 3–5 kanji with useful words, reviewed explanations, and mnemonics.

03

Build a usable sentence

Learn one grammar pattern using those words. Read sentence breakdowns, then construct or say the sentence yourself.

04

Practise pronunciation

Hear words individually and in complete sentences. Record, replay, inspect recognized transcripts and feedback, and retry.

05

Explore an interest in conversation

Enter a topic by typing or speaking, or use a lesson's suggestion. The AI begins a natural level-appropriate conversation. Qloo can enrich it or suggest scenarios/roleplay. Change the subject or finish whenever you want, with hints, translations, and corrections.

06

Keep it useful after the lesson

Get a recap and reference sheet with words, grammar, examples, and audio. Save useful words and phrases; return for due reviews.

Session completion begins learning. Mastery comes from consistent recall over time. Grammar and sentence use return for practice alongside individual words.

03

Your chosen topic takes priority

Choose interests during onboarding and update them whenever needed. Real Qloo results inform relevant cultural topics; the same free-conversation mode supports standalone and lesson-linked practice. Enter topics directly in chat; your current topic takes priority over saved interests. Lessons can suggest vocabulary, grammar, and an everyday goal.

04

A clear shared MVP

One complete product milestone for both events. Scope is based on user value, integration reliability, and demonstrable quality, assuming AI-assisted development.

NowThe complete learning experience+
  • Android learning appExpo / React Native, with a Next.js web landing page.
  • Account + placementEmail/password with verification, recovery and remembered sessions; predefined placement, adjustable recommendation, and editable onboarding interests.
  • Reviewed starter teachingFocused N5/N4 lessons, 3–5 kanji and one grammar pattern, with small kana groups and beginner support through audio, meaning, romaji, and selectable input.
  • Pronunciation practiceModel audio, recording playback, speech recognition, transcripts, feedback, and retries.
  • Qloo-personalized AI conversationOne free-conversation mode follows the chosen topic, with Qloo enrichment, optional scenarios/roleplay, hints, translations, and corrections. Lessons open this same mode.
  • Recap + reusable reference sheetWords, grammar, example sentences, and audio to revisit during the day.
  • Flashcards + spaced follow-upSave useful words and phrases; correctly schedule meaning and reading reviews and revisit sentence use.
  • Persistent learning progressSave learning data across Android sessions/devices. Active study time and meaningful-step streaks remain separate from mastery.
  • Optional WaniKani importFamiliar kanji/vocabulary inform practice; other language skills remain separate.
  • AI gateway + two providersRoute AI through Cloudflare AI Gateway; switch configured providers through an adapter without changing learning-feature code.
  • Useful during AI outagesLessons, flashcards, and saved progress remain usable when a provider is unavailable or its quota is exhausted.
  • Prepared offline studyCached/downloaded reviews, lessons, references, audio, recording/playback, and sentence drafts remain usable. Preserve pending AI feedback; synchronize without duplicates or changing original study dates.
  • Practical cost choicesPrefer free tiers and free AI options where workable.

CutExplicit exclusions+
  • A web/PWA learning appThe chosen learning platform is Android. Web serves as the landing page only.

05

What has to work for another learner

Product proof

Complete it. Keep it. Return.

  1. A newcomer signs in, completes placement, and chooses interests that they can later update.
  2. They finish a supported session and use its Japanese in the shared free-conversation mode. Save the ended chat separately from evidence of practising the lesson goal.
  3. They record and replay speech, inspect recognized transcripts, get feedback, and retry.
  4. They keep the reference sheet and save useful phrases.
  5. They return later with progress intact and correctly scheduled review items.
