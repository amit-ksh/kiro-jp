## Platform

android

## Stack

Learner-selected Expo/React Native, TypeScript and Turborepo; Convex with Convex Auth; Resend; Cloudflare AI Gateway with a shared provider adapter; real Qloo cultural-context tools. Next.js is a separate static landing page only. Use the requested get-convex monorepo starter and remove its Clerk/sample-note behavior.

## Users

English-speaking Japanese beginners and early learners, including people with no kana knowledge and WaniKani users whose grammar, listening and speaking must be assessed separately from imported kanji/vocabulary familiarity.

## Product Purpose

Help someone recognize useful Japanese, recall its meanings/readings, understand one grammar pattern, practise words and sentences aloud and use them in an interest-led conversation. Return later for meaningful spaced review with retained progress.

## Positioning

Connect kanji recall to useful grammar and freely chosen conversation topics. Real Qloo results inform cultural relevance; checked curriculum and demonstrated skills determine Japanese difficulty. Chat completion, lesson-goal evidence and long-term mastery are distinct.

## Operating Context

Short daily sessions on Android, with partial effort preserved and prepared study available offline. Home has one main Start today's session action. Five sections: Home, Lessons, Review, Conversation, Profile. The first setup saves answers automatically and offers honest placement uncertainty and optional personalization/import.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The approved spec and checklist define one complete shared MVP for both events. Initial content is a focused seven-template N5/N4 inventory, not a complete JLPT course. The learner approved a US$10 total Gemini development/demo budget, enforced by reservations. Other providers remain free-only, with no automatic paid fallback. Higher levels, full mock tests, continuous voice, pitch-accent scoring, subscriptions, iOS and social login are deferred. Hosted reset links and public distribution remain pending.

The app's name is Kiro JP, chosen by the learner. Use it in Android display branding, welcome/loading screens and the static landing page. The user requires the palette and overall layout character in references/j-image-main.webp, adapted to readable learning material. Preserve the approved product's simple English explanations and supported Japanese.

## Evidence on Hand

Approved scope, PRD/spec and build sequence; supplied UI references; installed early Android foundation; synchronized synthetic accounts; three bundled placement clips; two real Gateway-routed synthetic provider probes; owned immutable study-journal checks. These do not prove the full learning/chat/speech/review loop. Qloo access is pending, and no native-speaker reviewer, finished app or public release is claimed.

## Product Principles

- Teach first, then ask for active recall; keep meaning, word reading, kana literacy and speaking separate.
- Learner-chosen conversation topics take priority over saved interests.
- Preserve real effort without granting unrelated mastery or goal completion.
- A stale device cannot undo already-acknowledged review progress.
- Every unavailable activity retains progress and offers a useful next action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support kana readings, optional romaji/furigana/translations, audio replay and selectable input. Text sizing and Android font scaling must work; controls need accessible labels and at least 48dp targets. Typing remains available when speech fails. Do not assume kana fluency or require microphone access to continue.
